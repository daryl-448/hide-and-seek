## Notes (ours)

- Static mode: launderers draw every branch uniformly over its feasible options; innocents pay now, directly, in one output; both send change to a fresh address with probability `p_fresh`. The detector flags uniformly at random at the world's budget.
- Launderers receive new proceeds at the start of every episode, so laundering spans the world.
- Services queue a withdrawal to an innocent for every completed innocent payment to a service, so money keeps circulating; payouts wait at most `payout_max_wait` sub-steps for a full batch.
- The simulator's causal counts are each wallet's state at its world's last step; the real ones are at ts 29. A world spans 10 steps, so `c_steps` cannot reach the real range.
- `num_input_addresses` / `num_output_addresses` count inputs and outputs (spent and created outputs), which is never below the distinct-address link count, as audited on the real data.
